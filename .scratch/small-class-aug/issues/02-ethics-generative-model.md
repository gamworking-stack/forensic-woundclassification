# Does the ethics approval cover a generative model trained on patient photos

Type: task (HITL)
Status: open
Blocked by: none

## Question

The researcher checks the approved protocol and approval letter in `02_Ethics/` (expedited review), or asks the advisor or the ethics office:
1. Does the approval allow training a generative model on the patient photographs?
2. May synthetic crops be stored on Raptor and shown in the thesis?
3. If not, what amendment is needed, and how long does it take?

Resolved when the answer is recorded. It gates every 5B ticket; 5A does not wait.
